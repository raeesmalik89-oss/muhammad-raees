I write articles that connect my pharmacy expertise to AIOps — topics like "How AIOps Prevents Pharmacy Dispensing Errors." This positions me as unmissable to health-tech hiring managers who need both clinical and engineering depth.

STEP 02

Building in Public on GitHub

I am open-sourcing real healthcare AIOps tools — a drug alert classifier, a clinical observability dashboard, and a FHIR data pipeline. Demonstrated, working code is my strongest proof of skill.

STEP 03

Sharing Case Studies Weekly

I document every project as a case study: Problem → Approach → Result with real metrics. "Reduced false alerts by 60%" tells a hiring manager more than any list of tools ever could.

STEP 04

I am actively pursuing AWS Healthcare specialty, Dynatrace Professional, and Splunk Core certifications — each one a searchable keyword that puts my profile in front of recruiters looking for exactly this combination.

STEP 05

Engaging Health-Tech Communities

I engage daily with health-tech CTOs, clinical informatics leaders, and digital health communities — building a reputation as a credible voice at the intersection of AIOps and healthcare. Visibility precedes opportunity.

STEP 06

Reaching Out with Real Value

I reach out to engineering managers with specific clinical insights, not generic templates. My pharmacy background makes every message genuinely non-generic — a differentiator that opens conversations others cannot start.
